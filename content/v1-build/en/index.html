Starting point

The financial sector is moving towards a greater division of labour. Asset managers concentrate on strategy and markets, asset management companies and other institutions on their core business, and investors choose the partners that match their objectives. At the same time, the revised AIFM Directive and DORA are raising the requirements for outsourcing and service providers. What is needed are firms that take on regulatory tasks reliably and make their work transparent.

Who we work for

For all who bear responsibility

Our clients and partners invest on behalf of third parties, manage funds or secure pension commitments. Supervisory authorities and investors look closely at what they do. We take on the part of the work that requires expertise, diligence and robust processes.

Our role

Market knowledge meets a regulated framework

As an asset management company, we bear the legal responsibility for the funds we manage. As investment advisers, asset managers contribute their knowledge of markets and assets and support us in our investment decisions. In this way, we combine proximity to the market with a reliable regulatory framework.

Investment adviser

Advises with market knowledge

Investment advisers develop the strategy, identify suitable investments and make recommendations.

ServiceInvest

Decides and takes responsibility

We review the recommendations, take the decisions and are accountable for them to investors and the supervisory authority.

Together

Coordinated and transparent

Roles, processes and information channels are defined from the outset and are clear to everyone involved.

Partner funds

Your expertise, our regulated framework

Asset managers with a clear strategy and their own market access apply their expertise in funds that ServiceInvest launches and manages. They accompany these funds as investment advisers, from the selection of investments to the further development of the portfolio, and remain a visible point of contact for investors.

ServiceInvest takes the investment decisions and bears the regulatory responsibility. This creates a clear division of tasks that holds for the entire term of the fund.

Services

Services for the regulated financial sector

What we have built for our own funds we also make available to others: asset management companies, asset managers and investors as well as banks, insurers, pension institutions and financial service providers. They obtain individual services or entire areas of responsibility, on a permanent basis or for a limited period.

01

Fund and corporate accounting

Accounting for funds and asset-holding companies from a central data set, reconciled into financial reporting under investment law.

02

Risk management and controlling

Risk and liquidity management with limits, stress tests and clear, comprehensible reports.

03

Service provider management

Digitally supported management of service providers and support for outsourcing control. We set up the processes, accompany the ongoing monitoring and prepare the results for management, auditors and the supervisory authority.

04

Regulatory reporting

Reports to the Deutsche Bundesbank and the Federal Financial Supervisory Authority (BaFin) as well as data for the reporting obligations of institutional investors, for example under Solvency II or the CRR.

05

Investor reporting

Periodic reports for investors and committees, with the quantitative parts largely automated.

06

Digital solutions for regulation

Digitally supported services for compliance, anti-money laundering, outsourcing and ICT registers and further obligations. We set up the solution, accompany ongoing operations and ensure defined processes, deadlines and complete documentation.

Do your needs lie in between or beyond? Talk to us. We will be glad to examine how we can support you within the framework of supervisory law.

Technology

Digital from end to end

Our fund management runs digitally, from the receipt of data to the finished report. Data from investment advisers, depositaries and service providers flows in automatically, is checked and is used for all tasks. Systems handle the routine; our specialists concentrate on review and decision-making.

It is based on the technology of the Institutional Investment Group (2IG), which is continuously being developed.

01

Seamless data

Information is captured once, reconciled automatically and is available for all tasks in the same quality.

02

Automated controls

Plausibility, limit and deadline checks run in the system and report deviations immediately to those responsible.

03

Results on demand

We provide reports and key figures digitally. Every review and approval is documented and traceable at any time.
