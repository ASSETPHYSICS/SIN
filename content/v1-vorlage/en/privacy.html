Additional definitions:

Cookies and similar technologies

Cookies are text files that a website places on, or reads from, your terminal device. They contain combinations of letters and numbers in order, for example, to recognise the user and their settings when they reconnect to the website that set the cookie, to allow the user to stay logged in to a customer account, or to analyse certain usage behaviour statistically.

WebStorage technology makes it possible to store variables and values locally in the user’s browser cache. The technology comprises both “sessionStorage”, which remains stored until the browser tab is closed, and “localStorage”, which remains stored in the browser cache until the user clears the cache. Among other things, localStorage technology makes it possible to recognise the user and their settings when they access our website.
